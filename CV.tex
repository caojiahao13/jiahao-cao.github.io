\section{Current Research Interests}

My research aims at developing rigorous, interpretable, and computationally scalable statistical methods for complex structured data, with a particular emphasis on Bayesian inference, uncertainty quantification, and modern statistical machine learning. I connect this methodological work to applications in public health, environmental science, and social systems when the data are correlated (spatial or spatio-temporal), high-dimensional (including functional data), constrained (such as compositional or shape-constrained data), or incompletely measured (including measurement error and privacy-protected data).
